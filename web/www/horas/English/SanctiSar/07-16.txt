[Officium]
The Translation of St. Osmund, Bishop and Confessor

[Oratio]
{::English from Warren, The Sarum Missal in English (1911)::}
O God, Whose miracles of old we perceive to shine forth even in our days to the magnifying of Thy Name, and the praise and honour of S. Osmund, Confessor and Bishop: mercifully grant us, at the intercession of him whose Translation we keep, both to glorify Thee in this present world, and to enjoy Thy presence in that which is to come.
$Per Dominum
