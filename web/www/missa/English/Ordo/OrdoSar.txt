# Incipit
!While the Priest vests for Mass, let him say the following hymn:
v. Come, Creator-Spirit, all-divine, come visit every soul of thine, and fill with thy celestial flame the hearts which thou thyself didst frame.
!Which ended, he says:
V. Send forth thy Spirit, and they shall be created.
R. And thou shalt renew the face of the earth.
v. O God, to whom every heart is open and every desire known, and whom no secret escapes: purify by the infusion of the Holy Spirit the thoughts of our heart; that we may merit perfectly to love and worthily to praise thee. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.

!Let the Priest approach with his ministers to the step of the altar, and sign himself, saying:
v. In the Name of the Father, + and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.

!Then, joining his hands before his breast, he begins the Anthem:
S. I will go in unto the altar of God.
M. To God, who giveth joy to my youth.
!*&Introibo
!The Priest alternates with the ministers in reciting this psalm:
!Ps. 42, 1-5
S. Judge me, O God, and distinguish my cause from the nation which is not holy: deliver me from the unjust and deceitful man.
M. For Thou, O God, art my strength: why hast Thou cast me off? and why go I sorrowful whilst the enemy afflicteth me?
S. Send forth Thy light and Thy truth: they have conducted me and brought me unto Thy holy mount, and into Thy tabernacles.
M. And I will go in to the altar of God: to God who giveth joy to my youth.
S. To Thee, O God, my God, I will give praise upon the harp: why art thou sad, O my soul, and why dost thou disquiet me?
M. Hope in God, for I will still give praise to Him: the salvation of my countenance and my God.
S. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.
M. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.
!The Priest repeats the Anthem:
S. I will go in unto the altar of God.
M. To God, who giveth joy to my youth.

!Then let the Priest say the Confession, the deacon assisting on the right and the subdeacon on the left:
v. I confess to God, to Blessed Mary, to all the Saints, and to you: for I have sinned exceedingly in thought, word, and deed, by my fault. I pray Holy Mary, all the Saints of God, and you, to pray for me.
M. The Almighty God have mercy upon you, and pardon all your sins, deliver you from all evil, preserve and confirm you in good, and lead you to everlasting life.
S. Amen.
!Then let the ministers repeat the Confession, where the Priest said "you," the ministers say "thee, Father":
M. I confess to God, to Blessed Mary, to all the Saints, and to thee, Father: for I have sinned exceedingly in thought, word, and deed, by my fault. I pray Holy Mary, all the Saints of God, and thee, Father, to pray for me.
S. The Almighty God have mercy upon you, and pardon all your sins, deliver you from all evil, preserve and confirm you in good, and lead you to everlasting life.
S. Absolution and remission of all your sins, space for true repentance, amendment of life, grace, and the consolation of the Holy Spirit, the Almighty and merciful Lord grant unto you.
M. Amen.
!Then let the Priest say the Versicle:
V. Our help is in the name of the Lord.
R. Who made heaven and earth.
V. Blessed be the name of the Lord.
R. From this time, now, and for ever.

!*S
!Then let the Priest kiss the deacon, and afterwards the subdeacon (which is observed save only in Masses for the dead, and in the three fast days before Easter), saying:
v. Receive the kiss of peace and of love, that ye may be meet to perform the sacred offices at the holy altar.

!Then let the Priest approach to the altar, and say at the middle of the altar with inaudible voice, the body bowed and the hands joined:
v. Take away from us, we beseech thee, O Lord, all our iniquities: that we may be worthy to enter into the holy of holies with pure minds. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
!Then let the Priest raise himself up, kiss the altar in the middle, and sign himself on his face, saying:
v. In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, + and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.

!In the end of the Alleluia, or of the Sequence, or of the Tract, let the deacon, before he advances to read the Gospel, incense the middle of the altar only. Then, bowing to the Priest, let him say:

# Credo
!*&Credo
v. I believe in one God, the Father almighty, Maker of heaven and earth, and of all things visible and invisible. And in one Lord Jesus Christ, the only-begotten Son of God. And born of the Father, before all ages. God of God: Light of Light: true God of true God. Begotten, not made, consubstantial with the Father; by whom all things were made. Who, for us men, and for our salvation, came down from heaven. (Here kneel down.) And became incarnate by the Holy Ghost of the Virgin Mary: and was made man. He was crucified also for us, suffered under Pontius Pilate, and was buried. And the third day He rose again according to the Scriptures. And ascended into heaven, and sitteth at the right hand of the Father. And He shall come again with glory to judge both the living and the dead: of whose kingdom there shall be no end. And in the Holy Ghost, the Lord and Giver of life: who proceedeth from the Father and the Son. Who together with the Father and the Son is adored and glorified: who spoke by the prophets. And in one, holy, Catholic and Apostolic Church. I confess one baptism for the remission of sins. And I look for the resurrection of the dead. And the life of the world to come. Amen.

# Offertorium
!Then let the Priest, turned to the people, say:
V. The Lord be with you.
R. And with thy spirit.
!Then at the altar let the Priest say:
v. Let us pray.
&offertorium

!After the offertory, let the deacon hand the chalice with the paten and the host to the Priest. Then, bowing a little, let him elevate the chalice with both hands, offering the sacrifice to the Lord, saying:
v. Receive, O Holy Trinity, this oblation, which I, an unworthy sinner, offer in honour of thee, of the Blessed Virgin and all thy Saints, for my sins and offences, and for the salvation of the living and the rest of all the faithful departed. In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, let this new sacrifice be acceptable to almighty God.

!*S
!Then let him take the censer from the deacon and incense the sacrifice, saying:
v. Let my prayer, O Lord, be directed unto thee, as incense in thy sight.
!Then let the Priest be incensed, and afterwards the choir.

!These things finished, let the Priest go to the right horn of the altar and wash his hands, saying:
v. Cleanse me, O Lord, from all defilement of mind and body: that I may be able, made clean, to fulfil the holy work of the Lord.
!The hands washed, let him return to the altar, and with head and body bowed, and hands joined, say:
v. In the spirit of humility and in a contrite mind let us be received by thee, O Lord: and so let our sacrifice be made in thy sight this day, that it may please thee, O Lord God.
!And raising himself, let him kiss the altar on the right of the sacrifice, and sign himself, saying:
v. In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, + and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.
!Then let the Priest turn to the people, and with low voice say:
S. Pray, brethren and sisters, that my sacrifice and yours may be acceptable to the Lord our God.
M. May the grace of the Holy Spirit illuminate thy heart and thy lips, and may the Lord accept worthily this sacrifice of praise from thy hands for our sins and offences.
